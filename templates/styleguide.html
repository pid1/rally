Calendar month grid

Today is marked with a rule, never a fill: the palette is greyscale, so the only colour on the page is the family-member dots and a filled cell would shout over them. A day that overflows offers “+N more” and switches to that day’s agenda — a popover would be a second modal chassis. Below 768px the grid does not render at all: seven columns leave 42px per day, and no styling makes that carry a 44px target, so the calendar page shows the agenda instead.

Preparedness state and rows

The one place the palette leaves greyscale. State is encoded in colour and in words, because the meaning is the message: an expired water filter has to read as different in kind, not merely in wording. These pills report — they do not filter — so they are deliberately not .filter-chip.
